# Organizer Journey

This document outlines the intended organizer journey for managing the Atlanta Tennis Connect platform.

## 1. Login
- **Action**: Organizer accesses the login portal and enters their credentials.
- **Information needed**: Organizer email and password (mocked for now).
- *UNKNOWN*: Are there different tiers of organizers (e.g., super admin vs. league captain)?

## 2. Organizer dashboard
- **Action**: Organizer views the main dashboard.
- **Information needed**: Overview of active seasons, number of active leagues, recent registrations.

## 3. Seasons
- **Action**: Organizer manages the seasons.
- **Information needed**: Current, upcoming, and past seasons.
- *UNKNOWN*: Who creates seasons? Can they overlap?

## 4. Leagues
- **Action**: Organizer navigates to the leagues view.
- **Information needed**: List of all leagues, their capacity, and current registration status.
- *UNKNOWN*: Who creates leagues? Can anyone with an organizer role create one?

## 5. Create/edit league
- **Action**: Organizer creates a new league or edits an existing one.
- **Information needed**: League name, season, format, age group, skill level, location, dates, registration fee, capacity limits.
- *UNKNOWN*: Who can edit a published league once registrations have started?

## 6. View registrations
- **Action**: Organizer clicks on a league to see who has registered.
- **Information needed**: Player names, payment status, contact info.
- *UNKNOWN*: How are registrations approved? Is it automatic upon payment, or does the organizer manually approve?

## 7. Manage players
- **Action**: Organizer views the list of registered players for a league.
- **Information needed**: Player's NTRP skill level, email, phone number.

## 8. Other relevant organizer actions
- *UNKNOWN*: How are skill levels defined and enforced?
- *UNKNOWN*: Are organizers responsible for match scheduling, or is it automated?
- *UNKNOWN*: Are organizers responsible for scoring/results, or do players self-report?
